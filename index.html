Understanding VTA

Why does the
angle matter?

Your stylus reads the groove at a specific angle. Getting it exactly right — for each record — is the difference between good and extraordinary sound.

VTA
Vertical Tracking Angle

The angle the tonearm makes with the record surface when viewed from the side. Deviations of even fractions of a millimetre affect clarity, tonal balance and soundstage.

SRA
Stylus Rake Angle

The angle of the stylus contact relative to the groove — ideally 92°. When wrong, you'll hear harshness, reduced bass definition, and a collapsed soundstage.

FLY
On the Fly Adjustment

Easy VTA lets you adjust while the record plays. Hear the change in real time. Finding the "sweet spot" has never been this precise or enjoyable.

FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

Does VTA adjustment really make an audible difference?+
Yes — especially with high-quality line-contact or microline stylus profiles. The angle determines how the stylus tip contacts the groove wall. Even small variations cause measurable changes in distortion, tonal balance, and soundstage depth. Many audiophiles report VTA adjustment as one of the most significant improvements to their vinyl playback.
What makes Easy VTA different from other VTA adjusters?+
Easy VTA is engineered specifically for on-the-fly adjustment — you adjust while the record is playing and hear the change in real time. Two precision linear bearings create a solid, rattle-free mechanism. The custom micrometer with inverted analogue scale lets you make precise, repeatable adjustments and return to any known setting. All machined from aerospace-grade 6061 aluminium with military-spec anodizing.

Easy VTA currently supports: Rega RB-330 · Jelco SA-750D · Ortofon AS-309R · Sumiko Premier MMT · GrooveMaster · Thomas Schick · Sorane SA-1.2 / TA-1 · Clearaudio (Satisfy, Unify, Performance) · SME Sliding Base. Use the compatibility finder above or contact us if your tonearm isn't listed.
